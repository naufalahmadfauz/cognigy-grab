## 3. Common fixture and assessment contract

Every profile below inherits these conditions and its row-specific sources,
milestones, traceability and regression controls. A branch-local starting point
does not establish the omitted prefix of a complete original TC.

1. **Reference facts:** declare same-session Asia/Kuala_Lumpur (UTC+8), Tuesday
   2026-11-03, explicit cancellation age 14 days for older fixtures and 1 day for
   recent/correction controls. This uses agreed Q6 drafting conventions, not a new
   absolute-date/calendar policy or injectable runtime clock. TC-010 needs no age;
   the GM-only bypass must also run without age/loudspeaker prerequisites.
2. **Merchant facts versus evidence:** specify LOB, task, known preferences,
   current location and previous attempts. Distinguish author-declared fixture
   facts from facts actually communicated to the Agent. Never let a hidden fixture
   fact establish an Agent observation, consent, completed action or backend result.
3. **Pacing:** a `→` below is a dependency. For one-by-one, give one current
   action/question and **wait for its relevant response before advancing**.
   All-at-once may group supported navigation within a decision segment, but still
   waits at status, form visibility, retry result, submission report, consent and
   finish. Existing managed GF checkpoints must accept the appropriate commit;
   conversation recollection cannot manufacture one.
4. **Submission continuation:** actual visible form → details/evidence guidance
   appropriate to LOB/Q7 → merchant self-submission → explicit submission report →
   conditional acknowledgement → further-help/finish handling. Visibility is not
   submission, submission is not backend acceptance, approval or payment. For dual
   intent, observed status and remaining intent precede submission; submit-only
   TC-009 and applicable variants retain their supported direct entry.
5. **Consent ownership:** preserve task-specific guidance consent and separate
   platform fallback, form retry and person-offer responses. A correction does not
   replenish a spent retry or renew a declined offer. Q8's compatibility rule
   preserves only valid current-order progress and applicable disclosures.
6. **Policy/unknown outcomes:** no early eligibility/form promise, wrong-role
   inference from a missing screen, new recovery route, automatic escalation or
   successful-resolution claim. A supported boundary may be reached while the
   concern remains unresolved. Unestablished next remedies remain **UNKNOWN /
   BLOCKED_SPEC** for that outcome, not fabricated full-target success.
7. **Two independent axes:** behavior is assessed on the observed branch under
   applicable reviewed rules; coverage requires the declared trigger, observations
   and all intended milestones. A skipped failed action, different consent answer
   or justified alternate route cannot cover the intended recovery/decline/handoff
   target. Full coverage can coexist with a behavioral failure; unsupported policy
   is `BLOCKED_SPEC`, missing channel/configuration evidence may be `BLOCKED_ENV`
   or `NEEDS_REVIEW`. All current verdicts are **NOT_RUN**.
