## Interpretation and coverage cautions

- “Within/beyond” are workbook fixture labels, not an executed check of every
  weekday boundary. Actual date basis is an OD-005 review item.
- “UDR positive,” “Closing A” and “Closing Others” are retained source path
  labels. Their business interpretation is finish/further-help handling versus
  mock handoff, derived from the accompanying dialogue/capability. No acronym
  expansion or backend outcome is invented.
- A visible order, missing order, absent request link, absent compensation status
  and absent form are different facts. None alone proves eligibility, payment,
  wrong role, successful submission or failed navigation elsewhere.
- Original direct DF links come only from `WB:D!D5:D22`. Wider requirement-based
  impacts belong in the separate impact matrix. No direct DF links are recorded
  for TC-002 or TC-025; that is not evidence that these journeys are defect-free.
- `WB:S!G4:G18` counts historical In progress/Fixed (retest)/Open records; its
  cached total of 15 is not today's unresolved count. The original workbook and
  original Defect Log remain unchanged.
